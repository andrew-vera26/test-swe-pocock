Programming ahead of the midterms

CSAD doesn’t run partisan campaigns or endorse candidates. In a midterm election year, its role is to give the Kenyon community structured, civil spaces to think through the stakes of American democracy — through public lectures, panels, and student research — regardless of which party or candidate is on the ballot.

Nonpartisan, always. CSAD promotes nonpartisan civic and political discourse through deliberative inquiry and civil, probing conversation. It does not endorse candidates, parties, or legislation, including in election years.

Student research on local elections

Past CSAD Associates work has looked directly at election outcomes close to home: student associates Joseph Ferrari ’24 and Andrew Nguyen ’23 conducted an analysis of the November 2022 local elections in Knox County, part of CSAD’s broader effort to connect national democratic questions to local political life.
