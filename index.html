Open dialogue, nonpartisan discourse

The Center for the Study of American Democracy (CSAD) seeks “open dialogue toward a subtle understanding of history, timeless questions and fundamental principles.” Based in Oden Hall and founded in 2007 with a “We the People” Challenge Grant from the National Endowment for the Humanities, CSAD organizes conferences, lectures, and seminars that bring students, faculty, and visiting scholars into conversation about American democracy.

CSAD is nonpartisan. It promotes nonpartisan civic and political discourse through deliberative inquiry and civil, probing conversation. It does not endorse candidates, parties, or legislation.

What CSAD does

  • Sponsors lectures, panel discussions, and public events featuring visiting scholars, politicians, and journalists
  • Supports teaching and research opportunities for Kenyon faculty and students
  • Runs the CSAD Associates program, involving Kenyon students directly in research and event programming
  • Promotes student internships in Washington, D.C.

Learn more about the Center’s history and leadership on the About page, or see how CSAD approaches civic engagement ahead of this year’s midterm elections on the Midterm Election Work page.
