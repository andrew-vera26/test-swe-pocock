# CSAD Showcase Site

A 3-page unofficial static website highlighting the nonpartisan work of Kenyon College's Center for the Study of American Democracy (CSAD), with a focus on its programming in the lead-up to the midterm elections.

## Language

**CSAD**:
Kenyon College's Center for the Study of American Democracy, the real academic center this site describes. Founded 2007, based in Oden Hall.
_Avoid_: "the Center" alone without prior reference, "CSAD Kenyon"

**Nonpartisan**:
One word, no hyphen — matches CSAD's own usage. Describes the Center's commitment to civic and political discourse without endorsing candidates, parties, or legislation.
_Avoid_: "non-partisan" (hyphenated), "bipartisan" (these are not synonyms — bipartisan implies two sides cooperating; nonpartisan means taking no side)

**CSAD Associates**:
Kenyon students who collaborate with CSAD on research and programming (e.g. election analysis). Credited as collaborators on this site's content, not as site authors.
_Avoid_: "staff", "interns" (imprecise — Associates is the Center's own term for this role)

**Associate Director**:
Nancy Powers' title at CSAD, per Kenyon's own directory. Not "Director" — a 2018 leadership transition put David Rowe in as interim director while Powers continued as assistant/associate director; no source confirms her title has since changed to Director.
_Avoid_: "Director" (unverified for Powers; do not use without a source)

**Unofficial site**:
This project is not published by Kenyon College or CSAD, and carries no authorization from either. It is an independent overview of CSAD's public programming. The site must disclose this status (footer disclaimer) and link to the official CSAD site/contact for anyone wanting authoritative information.
_Avoid_: implying official affiliation, omitting the disclaimer
